Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

PubliX Group AB ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect through publixgroup.io, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and what rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Data controller

PubliX Group AB

Email: info@publixgroup.io

What data we collect

Contact form

When you submit the contact form we collect: name, email, company, message. Provided directly by you.

Newsletter

When you subscribe we collect: email address. Provided directly by you.

Analytics

We use Plausible Analytics, which collects only aggregated, anonymous statistics (page views, referring site, country, device type). No cookies. No personal identifiers. No cross-site tracking.

Server logs

Our hosting provider Netlify records standard HTTP access logs (IP address, timestamp, requested URL, user agent) for security and abuse prevention. Retained per Netlify's data policy.

Why we collect it (legal basis)

  • Contact enquiries — Legitimate interest (responding to your request). Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • Newsletter subscription — Consent (your explicit opt-in). Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. You can withdraw consent by unsubscribing at any time.
  • Server logs — Legitimate interest (site security). Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

How long we keep it

  • Contact enquiries: up to 24 months from last interaction
  • Newsletter subscriptions: until you unsubscribe
  • Server logs: per Netlify's retention (typically 30 days)
  • Analytics: aggregated, no per-user retention

Data processors

We use the following processors, each bound by GDPR data processing agreements:

  • Netlify, Inc. — website hosting and form submissions (US-based; EU-US Data Privacy Framework)
  • Plausible Insights OÜ — anonymous analytics (Estonia, EU)

We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers or marketing platforms.

Your rights under GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectify inaccurate data
  • Delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time (for consent-based processing)
  • Lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, email info@publixgroup.io. We will respond within 30 days.

Swedish supervisory authority: Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent change.
