## PubliX Group acquires Elected Technologies and enters the UK

Elected Technologies, maker of Caseworker, becomes the seventh company in PubliX Group and its first outside the Nordics. Simon McIntyre stays on as CEO.

[Read more](https://publixgroup.io/news/elected-technologies.html)
