## We believe in full transparency

### Will my company keep its own brand?

Yes. Your brand and identity are part of what makes your company valuable. We don't merge brands or fold you into a faceless group. You keep your name, your identity, and your place in the market.

### Will you change my product?

No. Product decisions stay with you and your team. We’ll offer AI capabilities, engineering support, and strategic input, but the roadmap is yours. We’re operators, not product managers.

### How long does the whole process take?

Typically 3–5 months from first conversation to close. We don’t rush, but we don’t drag things out either. The timeline depends largely on due diligence complexity and your own readiness.

### How do you value my company?

We use a transparent, market-based methodology that considers recurring revenue, growth trajectory, market position, customer quality and team strength. You’ll see the full model, with no black boxes. We present our valuation in person so you can challenge every assumption.
