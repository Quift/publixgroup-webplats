PubliX Group partners with Embrace Safety

A powerful SaaS platform enabling knowledge-based crime prevention and safety promotion for municipalities and local authorities.

STOCKHOLM, 5 March 2026 — PubliX Group partners with Embrace Safety, whose platform EMBRACE enables knowledge-based crime prevention and safety promotion for municipalities and local authorities. Embrace Safety was founded in Örebro in 2018 by Henrik Andershed, professor of criminology at Örebro University, and is led by CEO Johan Rydberg.

EMBRACE combines analytical software with continuous knowledge support: municipalities, police authorities and organisations use it to map and share situation reports on crime and insecurity, analyse causes, and document and follow up preventive measures. Several hundred organisations use the platform today.

Demand is driven by law: since 2023, Swedish municipalities have a statutory responsibility for crime prevention work under lag (2023:196), including producing recurring situation reports, exactly the workflow EMBRACE supports.

"Right from our first contact, we felt a strong trust in the buyer. They treated us with respect, asked thoughtful, sensible questions and really listened to our answers. We also felt that PubliX offered a structure — a group of companies that all contribute to societal value," said Johan Rydberg, CEO of Embrace Safety.

Embrace Safety continues under its own brand with its own team, and gains sister companies with standing relationships across Nordic public-sector markets.

About Embrace Safety

Embrace Safety is a Swedish SaaS company founded in Örebro in 2018. Its platform EMBRACE supports knowledge-based crime prevention and safety promotion for municipalities, police authorities and organisations.
