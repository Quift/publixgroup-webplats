PubliX Group enters Finland through partnership with Aspicore

A full-service software provider within grant management, marking PubliX Group's expansion into the Finnish market.

STOCKHOLM, 2 September 2025 — PubliX Group enters Finland through a partnership with Aspicore, a full-service software provider within grant management. Aspicore's cloud platform Apuraha4 supports foundations, organisations, unions, universities and public authorities through the complete lifecycle of grants — from application to payment.

Aspicore, led by CEO Harri Tanner, builds on two decades of work with grant systems. Apuraha4 is used by 140 customer organisations, which together manage more than EUR 100 million in grants annually.

The partnership marks PubliX Group's first step outside Sweden. Together with digiPlant, Aspicore forms the basis of a leading pan-Nordic challenger within grant management software.

About Aspicore

Aspicore is a Finnish full-service software provider within grant management. Its platform Apuraha4 is used by 140 customer organisations, managing more than EUR 100 million in grants annually.
