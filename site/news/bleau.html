PubliX Group acquires Bleau A/S and enters the Danish market

Bleau, the Aarhus company behind moliri, becomes the sixth company in PubliX Group and its first in Denmark. It keeps its name and its management.

STOCKHOLM, 15 September 2026 — PubliX Group has acquired Bleau A/S, the Danish software company behind moliri, a platform for secure and accessible digital communication in the public sector. Bleau is the group's sixth company and its first in Denmark, alongside three companies in Sweden and two in Finland.

Founded in Aarhus in 2008 as a management buy-out from Dynamicweb Software, Bleau moved from web agency to software house through the financial crisis. Its platforms are used by Danish municipalities and by social housing organisations: moliri for public-sector communication, NemBolig for the housing sector. Around 22 Danish municipalities, 35 social housing organisations and 250 critical infrastructure organisations use Bleau's platforms. Both are built on the same idea, that content is checked for accessibility, security and compliance at the point of publishing instead of being audited long afterwards.

The requirements Bleau builds against keep tightening. Danish municipalities came inside the scope of the NIS2 implementation in July 2025, and the European Accessibility Act has applied since June 2025.

"Denmark has been on our list since we set the acquisition strategy, and Bleau is the company I wanted in it," said Alexander Hübel, CEO of PubliX Group. "Robert and Peter have built a business that municipalities trust with the part of their communication that has to be right, and they have done it with a small engineering team and a closeness to their customers that you cannot buy in later. That is what we are buying, and it is why we intend to leave it alone."

Robert Filtenborg remains CEO of Bleau and Peter Terkildsen remains CTO, and both reinvest alongside PubliX. Customers keep the same agreements and the same product plan, and PubliX does not merge its companies.

The group's central team works with each company on public procurement, IT security and compliance, pricing and AI. Bleau also gains sister companies with standing relationships in neighbouring markets: Embrace Safety works with more than 75 Swedish municipalities, Koivu Solutions with Finnish healthcare and care organisations, and Aspicore with Finnish foundations and universities. Where moliri fits those markets, Bleau has a route in that it did not have on its own, and PubliX will move at a pace Bleau's customers can absorb.

"Bleau's own line, 'Powered by AI – Trusted by People', is close to how I think about it: automate the routine work, keep a person accountable for what reaches the citizen," said Alexander Hübel on the first area of joint work. "We will put group resources behind that work from the start, and it runs both ways. What Bleau has learned about validating content before it is published belongs in the other companies too."

"By choosing PubliX Group we have made sure that Bleau remains Bleau," said Robert Filtenborg, CEO of Bleau A/S. "Our employees carry on with the work they do on our customers' digital solutions, and our strategy and development plans for moliri are unchanged. The difference is that we now have greater strength to grow our development community and reach even further with moliri, nationally and internationally. Even though it is early in the process, we are already looking at initiatives between the companies in PubliX Group that can advance our interests, both in Denmark and in the other countries where the group operates."

PubliX Group continues to look for founder-led software companies in the Nordics and Northern Europe that run regulated public-sector workflows.

About Bleau A/S

Bleau A/S is a Danish software company founded in Aarhus in 2008. Bleau develops moliri, a compliance-driven SaaS platform for digital communication in the public sector, and NemBolig, a platform for Danish social housing organisations.
