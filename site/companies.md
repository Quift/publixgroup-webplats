Our companies

# Founder-led, mission‑driven

Every company in the PubliX portfolio was built by founders who care deeply about public services. They chose to join us and stayed to keep building.

Social care

### Tidvis

Tidvis provides an integrated business system for the social care sector: scheduling, time reporting, payroll and documentation in one platform for personal assistance and LSS providers. Their tools are designed by people who understand the daily reality of care work.

Adam Hjort

CEO

Stockholm, Sweden [tidvis.se](https://tidvis.se/)

Grant management

### digiPlant

digiPlant develops SBS Manager, an application management platform for foundations, sponsors and public organisations, from receiving applications to review, decision and disbursement. Easier applications, shorter handling times and more grants paid out.

Alexander Törnqvist

Co-Founder & CEO

Örebro, Sweden [digiplant.se](https://www.digiplant.se/sv/)

Foundation management

### Aspicore

Aspicore builds digital platforms for foundation and grant management, helping public-benefit organisations process applications, manage grants and ensure transparent governance.

Harri Tanner

CEO

Helsinki, Finland [aspicore.com](https://aspicore.com/)

Healthcare staffing

### Koivu Solutions (Sotender)

Koivu Solutions develops Sotender, a mobile-first platform that connects healthcare workers with open shifts and reduces reliance on staffing agencies. The wellbeing services county of Ostrobothnia has reported annual savings of more than EUR 7 million.

Janne Salmi

Co-Founder & CEO

Helsinki, Finland [sotender.fi](https://sotender.fi/)

Crime prevention

### Embrace Safety

Embrace Safety develops EMBRACE, a platform for knowledge-based crime prevention and safety promotion. Municipalities, police authorities and organisations use it to map situation reports, analyse causes and follow up preventive measures.

Johan Rydberg

CEO & Head of Sales

Örebro, Sweden [embracesafety.se](https://www.embrace-safety.se/)

Public communication

### Bleau

Bleau develops moliri, a compliance-driven platform for secure and accessible digital communication in the public sector, and NemBolig for Danish social housing organisations. Content is checked for accessibility, security and compliance at the point of publishing.

Robert G. Filtenborg

CEO

Aarhus, Denmark [bleau.dk](https://www.bleau.dk/)

Casework & correspondence

### Elected Technologies

Elected Technologies develops Caseworker, a casework and correspondence system for elected representatives and their offices, used in 16 parliaments around the world. CaseworkerGov does the same for councils and public bodies, and VoterID handles constituency and membership management for MPs and political parties.

Simon McIntyre

Founder & CEO

Shipston-on-Stour, UK [electedtechnologies.com](https://www.electedtechnologies.com/)

## What happens after you join

Joining PubliX is designed to be straightforward. This is what the first year looks like for a typical portfolio company.

### Day one: Nothing changes

Your team, your office, your customers. We're not here to reorganise.

2

### Months 1–3: Foundations

We work together on economics, pricing, compliance and security.

3

### Months 3–12: Growth

Cross-sell to shared customers, open new countries, add adjacent workflows.

4

### Year 1+: Platform value

Share in the collective growth of the group. Your success compounds.
